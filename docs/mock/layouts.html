Layout 1 · Stacked (default)

Place List on the left; timetable in multi-day view above the map. Senso-ji is selected, so it is highlighted in all three panes. The map shows the focus day (12/02) as numbered stops; other places are grey dots. The map shows every other place as a grey pin with its type icon (Forget places are hidden); chips at the top choose which days and types to highlight. The walk leg follows the streets; train legs stay straight lines until a Japanese transit source is chosen. In the timetable, the highlighted grips show where to drag to resize: the edge of a day header (width) and an hour edge in the time column (height); the scrollbars show it scrolls both ways. Travel labels show the mode icon; 12/03 has a walk suggestion ("· walk 5 min?"), and 12/05 uses Drive as its day default.

Dragging "Ueno museum" from the Place List onto 12/03 at 13:45. Its closed hours are hatched in every column (it is closed on Fridays, so 12/04 is hatched all day). Fixed blocks show a lock and their reason; Omoide Yokocho on 12/04 has a warning because it is scheduled before opening time.
